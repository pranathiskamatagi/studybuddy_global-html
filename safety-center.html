Safety Center

Your safety comes first

A few things to keep in mind while learning and teaching with people around the world.

Keep sessions on the platform

Chat, voice, and video should all stay inside Learnora, not on outside apps.

Never share personal details

Avoid sharing your address, financial info, or passwords with anyone you meet here.

Report anything that feels off

Use the flag icon during a chat session any time behavior makes you uncomfortable.

Block whenever you need to

Blocking is immediate and permanent - that person won't be matched with you again.
