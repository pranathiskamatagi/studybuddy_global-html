Terms & Conditions

Last updated: August 2026

1. Be respectful

Learnora connects students from around the world to learn and teach together. Treat every study partner with the same respect you'd want in a classroom - no harassment, hate speech, or inappropriate content, ever.

2. Keep it on the platform

Chat, voice, and video sessions should stay inside Learnora. Don't ask other users to move conversations to outside apps, and never share personal contact details, passwords, or financial information.

3. Reporting and blocking

If someone makes you uncomfortable, use the Report or Block option available in every chat session. Our safety team reviews every report - see the Safety Center for more details.

4. Academic honesty

Learnora is here to help you understand concepts, not to complete graded assignments, quizzes, or exams for you. Use sessions to learn, not to cheat.

5. Coins, badges, and ratings

Coins, diamonds, and badges are for fun and recognition only - they have no cash value and can't be exchanged, transferred, or sold.

6. Account suspension

We may suspend or remove accounts that violate these terms, including repeated reports, abusive behavior, or attempts to bypass safety features.

7. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms occasionally. Continuing to use Learnora after a change means you accept the updated terms.

8. Contact us

Questions about these terms? Reach out through the Help & Support section any time.
