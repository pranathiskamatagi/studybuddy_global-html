How to explain effectively

A few quick techniques to help your session go smoothly.

1

Start with their level 🧩

Ask "What do you already know about this?" Don't assume they know the basics.

2

Explain simply 💡

Use easy words and break the topic into small steps. Avoid complicated terms unless you explain them.

3

Show an example ✏️

Give a simple example or connect the concept to real life.

4

Let them try 🎯

Ask them a question or give a small problem. Don't give the answer immediately.

💬 Remember

"A great teacher doesn't just give answers. They help someone understand."
