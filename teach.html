Key Concept

Algebra is the branch of math that uses letters and symbols to represent numbers and quantities in formulas and equations. The core idea is finding the value of an unknown — usually called x — that makes an equation true.

Worked Example

Solve for x: 2x + 6 = 14

  1. Subtract 6 from both sides: 2x = 8
  2. Divide both sides by 2: x = 4
  3. Check: 2(4) + 6 = 14 ✓

Common Mistakes

Students often forget to apply the same operation to both sides of the equation, or mix up the order of operations when simplifying. Always undo addition/subtraction before multiplication/division.

How to Explain It Simply

Think of an equation like a balanced scale — whatever you do to one side, you must do to the other to keep it balanced. This analogy helps learners visualize why we perform the same operation on both sides.
